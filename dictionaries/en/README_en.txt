English Hunspell Spellchecker Dictionary
-----------------------------------------

Source: dictionary-en (https://github.com/wooorm/dictionaries), based on the
en_US Hunspell dictionary distributed with LibreOffice.

Used the same way as dictionaries/ar: loaded with nspell to provide a custom,
platform-independent English spellchecker for the note editor, so English and
Arabic checking behave consistently on every OS (see electron-main.cjs /
server.ts).
